  Cardiac Services

Comprehensive Heart Care Services

Advanced interventional and diagnostic cardiology procedures using the latest technology and techniques.

2D ECHO, Stress ECHO & TMT

Ultrasound imaging and stress testing to assess heart structure, function, and response to physical exertion — key for diagnosing heart disease early.

Learn more

Coronary Angiography / Angioplasty

Minimally invasive procedure to visualise coronary arteries and open blocked vessels using a balloon catheter — restoring normal blood flow to the heart.

Learn more

Complex Angioplasty (IVL, Rotablation)

Specialised techniques including Intravascular Lithotripsy (IVL) and Rotablation for treating severely calcified or complex coronary blockages.

Learn more

FFR (Fractional Flow Reserve)

A pressure wire test to precisely measure the significance of a coronary blockage and guide whether stenting is truly necessary — reducing unnecessary procedures.

Learn more

Imaging-Guided Angioplasty (OCT / IVUS)

Optical Coherence Tomography (OCT) and Intravascular Ultrasound (IVUS) provide high-resolution 3D imaging inside coronary arteries for precise stent placement.

Learn more

Peripheral Angiography / Angioplasty

Diagnosis and treatment of blocked arteries in the legs and arms, helping restore blood flow and reduce symptoms like leg pain or numbness.

Learn more

Renal Angioplasty

Treatment of narrowed renal (kidney) arteries to improve blood pressure control and preserve kidney function through minimally invasive catheter techniques.

Learn more

Pacemaker / CRT / AICD Implantation

Implantation of permanent pacemakers, Cardiac Resynchronisation Therapy (CRT) devices, and Automated Implantable Cardioverter-Defibrillators (AICD) for rhythm disorders.

Learn more

Balloon Valvuloplasty

A catheter-based procedure to widen narrowed heart valves — an alternative to open-heart surgery for selected patients with valvular stenosis.

Learn more

Holter & ABPM Monitoring

24–48 hour ambulatory ECG (Holter) and Ambulatory Blood Pressure Monitoring (ABPM) to detect intermittent arrhythmias and blood pressure patterns in daily life.

Learn more

Preventive Cardiology

Personalised risk assessment, lifestyle guidance, and medical management to reduce the risk of heart attack, stroke, and other cardiovascular events before they happen.

Learn more

An Interventional Cardiologist is a heart specialist with additional training in catheter-based procedures such as angioplasty, stenting, pacemaker implantation, and structural heart interventions — without requiring open-heart surgery. They treat the same conditions as general cardiologists but can perform these minimally invasive procedures in a catheterisation laboratory.
